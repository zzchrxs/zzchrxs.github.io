About Me

I'm a Robotics Engineering student passionate about building systems that bridge the gap between hardware and software. My interests span digital logic design, embedded systems, robotics, and autonomous systems.

I enjoy designing processors from the gate level up and programming drones to navigate autonomously. Whether it's writing Verilog for a custom CPU or developing ROS 2 nodes for autonomous flight, I'm driven by the challenge of making complex systems work together seamlessly.

When I'm not engineering, I'm exploring new technologies and working on personal projects that challenge my understanding of how things work at a fundamental level.
